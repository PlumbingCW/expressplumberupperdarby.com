Older homes, converted apartments and shared lines

When a Victorian is split into apartments, one unit's leak can show up in another. We trace the source before repairing, and our leak detection process uses moisture readings and pressure tests so you fix the right pipe. If you are a landlord, tell us who will give access.

Some of the oldest sewer laterals in town are clay or cast iron, which can crack or collect roots over decades. A camera inspection on the sewer inspection page shows what the pipe looks like before anyone digs.
