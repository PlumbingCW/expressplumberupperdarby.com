Tankless Water Heater Service in Upper Darby, PA

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows instead of storing it, which saves floor space and ends the problem of running out, but it needs the right gas line, venting and yearly maintenance.

What a tankless conversion changes in the house

A tankless gas unit demands a lot of gas at once, often more than the old tank burner did. The existing gas line may be too small, and the vent is almost always different, using stainless or plastic venting through a wall. We measure both before quoting, because they decide whether the job is simple or large.

The water side matters as well. Tankless units need a certain flow to fire, so very low flow fixtures can cause a cold sandwich effect. We explain that with your actual fixtures in mind.

Installing a tankless unit outside

Outdoor tankless units are rated for exterior mounting and free up basement space. They need freeze protection for the water lines and the unit in cold weather, and a protected location away from windows and air intakes. In Pennsylvania winters, an outdoor install without freeze protection is a risk we do not take, and we tell you when a basement or indoor wall is the safer location.

Flushing and descaling, the maintenance that matters

Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger when hard water is heated. It narrows the passages and the unit starts showing error codes, short cycling or low flow. A flush with a descaling solution and a pump removes it. Most manufacturers call for periodic flushing, and the interval depends on your water and how much you use.

We do not state a hardness figure for Upper Darby, because it varies by system and by year. Test your tap with a hardness strip or ask your water utility for its annual water quality report, and we will set the flush interval from what you find.

A water pump for a tankless water heater

A recirculation pump keeps a loop of hot water moving so you do not wait for it at a far bathroom. On a tankless system it is installed with a return line or a crossover valve and a timer or sensor. It costs less to run than leaving a tank hot all day, but it adds a part that needs maintenance, and we explain the trade-off.

Repair or replace a tankless unit

Fault codes often point to simple items: a clogged inlet screen, a blocked condensate line, or scale. When the heat exchanger itself has failed or a control board is out of parts, replacement is the honest answer. If your tank-style unit is the problem, read about water heater repair instead.

Sizing a tankless unit by demand

A tank is sized by gallons. A tankless unit is sized by flow, the number of gallons per minute you need at a given temperature rise. A house with two showers, a dishwasher and a washer running together needs more flow than one with a single bathroom. The incoming water temperature in winter also matters, because colder water takes more heat to raise.
