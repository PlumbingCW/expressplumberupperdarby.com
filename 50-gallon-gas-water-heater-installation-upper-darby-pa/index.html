50 Gallon Gas Water Heater Installation in Upper Darby, PA

A 50 gallon gas tank is the standard size for many families, and replacing one is routine work for a plumber, but several details decide whether the new unit is safe and lasts.

This guide explains what happens on install day and what to ask before you agree to a price.

Is 50 gallons the right size?

Fifty gallons suits many households of three to five people. A larger family with several baths running at once may want a bigger tank or a high-recovery model. A small household may do fine with less. Size is about peak demand, not total usage, so think about the busiest morning of the week.

What happens on install day

  1. The plumber shuts off the gas and water, drains the old tank and disconnects the vent, gas line and water lines.
  2. The old tank is removed, and the floor and drain pan are checked.
  3. The new tank is set, leveled and connected with new flexible connectors and a new gas shutoff where needed.
  4. The vent is reconnected with the right slope and secure joints.
  5. A relief valve and discharge pipe are installed, and an expansion tank is added where required.
  6. The tank is filled, the burner is lit and the gas joints and vent are checked for leaks and draft.

The plumber should leave you with the temperature set, the shutoff locations shown and the manual in your hands. Ask for the model and serial number for your records.

Venting and combustion air

A gas water heater burns fuel, so its exhaust has to leave the house. A vent that slopes the wrong way, has loose joints or terminates badly can let carbon monoxide into living spaces. Gas appliances also need enough air to burn properly, and a heater in a tight closet may not get it.

Working carbon monoxide alarms on each floor are an important backstop. If an alarm sounds, get outside and call 911. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first. We handle the installation side, and safety work by the utility takes priority.

Gas line, shutoff and pressure relief

The gas line must be sized for the new burner, and every joint tested for leaks. A manual shutoff valve near the heater lets you stop gas quickly. The temperature and pressure relief valve is a safety device that opens if the tank overheats or pressure climbs, and it must have a discharge pipe pointing down to a safe place.

A drain pan under the tank catches a slow leak. A thermal expansion tank absorbs pressure when water heats up in a closed system, which many houses now have because of backflow devices on the supply. Our water heater installation page lists what we include in a scope.

Permits and licensing in Upper Darby

Upper Darby Township has adopted the Pennsylvania Uniform Construction Code and requires permits for residential alterations and repairs. Permit rules vary by job, so ask the township Department of Licenses and Inspection what applies to yours.

Upper Darby Township Code Chapter 265 says plumbing contractors working in the township must employ a licensed master plumber, and only the master plumber can apply for plumbing permits. Plumbing licenses in the township expire on March 31 each year.

Pennsylvania also requires contractors who do at least $5,000 a year in home improvement work to register with the Attorney General. You can look a contractor up at hicsearch.attorneygeneral.gov. Registration is not an endorsement, but it is a fair first check.

Questions to ask your installer

  • Is the price in writing, and does it include haul-away and permits?
  • What size is the gas line, and does it need to change?
  • Will you replace the vent, the connectors and the relief valve?
  • Is an expansion tank needed on this house?
  • What is the warranty, and who handles it?

Signs it is time to replace instead of repair

A tank that is leaking from the body cannot be repaired. A heater more than ten to fifteen years old that needs a major part is a candidate for replacement. Rusty water from the hot tap only, a rumbling sound and an unreliable pilot are other signs. If the heater is failing now, our water heater repair team can tell you honestly which path makes sense.
