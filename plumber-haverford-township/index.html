A larger township with many neighborhoods

Haverford Township has about 50,431 residents across 9.95 square miles, and Havertown sits entirely inside it. Communities include Llanerch, Brookline, Oakmont and Manoa. The township is larger than most boroughs we serve, and its housing ranges from early twentieth-century homes to postwar neighborhoods.

The township borders Upper Darby, and several creeks cross it: Cobbs Creek, Darby Creek, Naylors Run and Meadowbrook Run. We do not claim any street floods, but we ask where the water is coming from on every basement call.

Plumbing across different eras of housing

Houses built before the war often have galvanized or early copper supply lines and cast iron drains. Postwar tracts tend to have copper supply and slab or basement layouts that differ from older homes. We ask the age and type of the house first, because it shapes the likely cause.
