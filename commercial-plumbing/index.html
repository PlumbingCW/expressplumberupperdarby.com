Who we work for in a commercial building

Commercial plumbing here means small and mid-size properties: storefronts, restaurants, offices, mixed-use buildings with apartments above shops, and landlords with several units. The commercial core around Terminal Square and the 69th Street Transportation Center, and the shopping corridors on State Road and Garrett Road, are made up of exactly these buildings.

We do not claim to handle every large industrial job. We say so on the first call if a project is outside what we do, so you do not lose a day.

After-hours emergencies in a business

The most common after-hours calls are a leaking water heater in a back room, a backed-up floor drain in a kitchen, a burst supply line in a storage area and a toilet that has overflowed in a shared restroom. Each one can close a shop, so we ask three questions: what is leaking, can you shut it off, and what is at risk of water damage.

Where the problem is a drain, the drain cleaning and hydro jetting pages explain the tools. Where it is a pipe, burst pipe repair covers the repair and shutoff steps.

Landlords and property managers

If you manage rentals, you may call on behalf of a tenant. Tell us who will let us in, which unit is affected and whether the tenant has been told. Having access arranged saves hours, and we can send a written scope to you rather than the tenant.

For multi-unit properties, a leak in one unit often becomes a leak in three. Our leak detection methods trace where water starts so you repair the right unit, and moisture readings document the extent of damage for your records and for insurers.

Backflow testing and grease traps

Some commercial properties have backflow preventers that need periodic testing and grease traps that need cleaning. Requirements come from the local water utility and the township, and they vary by property. Ask Upper Darby Township and your water utility what applies to your building, and tell us what you find. We explain how these tests and cleanings work, and we say when a task is outside our scope.
