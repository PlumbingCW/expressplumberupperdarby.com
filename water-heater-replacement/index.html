When a failed tank cannot be repaired

A water heater tank is a steel vessel with a glass lining and a sacrificial anode rod that corrodes first. When the rod is spent and the lining cracks, the steel rusts through and water escapes from the body. No weld, sealant or part stops that. Replacement is the only answer.

Signs that point this way: water pooling at the base of the tank, rust-colored water from the hot tap only, or a tank over about ten years old that has started to rumble and lose capacity. If you are not sure whether yours has failed, water heater repair is the first step because a failed part is a much cheaper fix.

Sizing and fuel choices before we swap

A swap in the same location with the same fuel is the quickest path. A 40 gallon tank suits a one or two person household, while a family of four often does better with 50 gallons or a high recovery model. We size by how many people shower in the morning, not by the label on the old tank.

What a replacement visit includes

  1. 1

    Shut down

    We close the supply, cut power or gas and drain the old tank.

  2. 2

    Remove

    The old unit is disconnected and carried out for disposal.

  3. 3

    Set and connect

    The new tank is set, piped, vented and fitted with a new pressure relief valve and discharge line.

  4. 4

    Test

    We check for leaks, flame or element operation, venting and water temperature.

Before we leave you will see the new unit running, hear where the shutoff valves are, and know where the temperature setting sits. If the house had no expansion tank on a closed system, we explain what that does and quote it separately rather than slipping it in.

Replacement in older basements around Upper Darby

Water heaters in older homes in Drexel Hill, Beverly Hills and Kirklyn often sit near a floor drain, a laundry tub or an old boiler. Flue size and the path to a chimney can limit your options, and old shutoff valves may not close fully. We check them before the job and replace any that fail.

A basement that has flooded before, especially near the Cobbs Creek side of the township, deserves a unit raised off the floor on a stand. We mention this when we see signs of previous water, and the basement waterproofing page covers the larger question of keeping the space dry.

Gas safety on any replacement

If you smell gas at any time, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before calling a plumber. We never promise a response time for a gas smell. On a gas swap we test the connections for leaks and confirm the flue draws before we leave.

A cramped closet or a house that is converting away from gas opens up other choices. We lay the options side by side and price each one before you commit.

What happens to the old unit

A tank full of water and sediment is heavy, awkward and usually in a tight space. We drain it, disconnect it and carry it out, and we say in the scope whether haul-away is included. We also look at the floor and the drain pan underneath, because a tank that has leaked for a while can leave rust, soft flooring or a stained slab behind it.

Warranty and paperwork

Tanks come with manufacturer warranties that cover specific parts for a specific time, and the terms differ by model. We give you the model and serial number after the install and explain what the warranty does and does not cover. Keep the paperwork where you can find it, because a claim needs the serial number.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Upper Darby Department of Licenses and Inspection what applies to your job. In the township, only the licensed master plumber of a contracting company can apply for plumbing permits, and we explain which parts of a replacement usually need one.

Planning a replacement before the failure

The best time to replace a water heater is before it fails. A planned swap lets you choose the unit, compare prices and schedule the work at a good hour. A failure at night in January leaves you choosing from whatever can be delivered the next day. If your tank is more than ten years old, or has started to rumble or weep around the base, it is worth a call. See water heater installation for planned work.
