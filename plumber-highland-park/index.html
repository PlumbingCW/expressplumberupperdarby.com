Where Highland Park is

Highland Park is one of the neighborhoods listed within Upper Darby Township. Cobbs Creek Park and a golf course across the city line touch it on the east. A real estate guide to the neighborhood describes West Chester Pike as the dividing line, with Observatory Hill Playground on the north side and Sellers Park beside the Upper Darby Library on the south side. Neither the township nor the guide gives an official boundary, so we use those anchors rather than draw one.

The area is named for its history as well. The Flower and Clark Observatory stood here from 1897 to 1956, and the site is now Observatory Park. Local churches and schools, including St. Laurence, and Fire Company 26 are based here.

Housing, as described locally

The same real estate guide says most homes north of the Pike are classic Philadelphia-style rowhouses and townhouses. South of the Pike it describes a mix of Cape Cods, Four Squares and Tudor Revival houses, with a median year built of 1930. That is a marketing description, not a survey, so treat it as a guide only.

We do not assume what is inside the walls from a build year. A 1930 house may have been repiped, and a newer one may not. We look at the actual pipe on every visit.

In attached houses, a drain shared with the next house can push water into the lowest fixture when it clogs. If the basement floor drain gurgles, stop running water and call. This is general plumbing guidance, and we are not saying it happens more often in Highland Park than elsewhere.

While you wait

  • Find the main shutoffIt is usually where the water line enters the house near the meter. Turn it clockwise to stop a leak.
  • Open the lowest faucetThis drains the lines and lowers pressure while you wait.
  • Stay clear of wet electricsKeep away from outlets and the panel if the floor is wet.
  • Protect what you canLift rugs and boxes off the floor and take photos of any damage.
  • Have your address readyGive the street, the nearest cross street and the floor or unit if you are in Highland Park.
