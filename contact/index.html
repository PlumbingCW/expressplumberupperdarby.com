How to reach us

  • Phone, 24/7: (215) 714-6692
  • Email, non-urgent questions: contact@expressplumberupperdarby.com
  • Address: 32-36 Garrett Rd, Upper Darby, PA 19082

For anything urgent, call. Email is not monitored as closely, so use it for scheduling a planned project or for a question that can wait.

What to tell us when you call

  1. Your address and the nearest cross street.
  2. What you see: where the water is, what you hear and what stopped working.
  3. Whether you can shut off the water at the main.
  4. How old the house or the plumbing is, if you know.
  5. The best number to reach you, and who can let us in.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first. After the utility clears the house, call us.
