Where Primos is

Primos is one of the neighborhoods named within Upper Darby Township. The SEPTA Media/Wawa Line runs through the southern part of the township and has a Primos station. A Free Library branch and St. Eugene School are in the area, and the township's Fire Company 74 covers Primos, Secane and Westbrook Park together.

We could not find a published boundary for Primos or a housing survey for it, so we do not say how old its houses are or what pipes they have. We start with your address and what you see.

Why the address matters here

Names and ZIP codes overlap in this part of Delaware County. Secane is shared between Upper Darby and Ridley Township, and its ZIP code, 19018, is also the Clifton Heights ZIP code. People often mix them up. Primos and Secane are named together by the same fire company.

When you call, give the street name, the nearest cross street and the municipality on your tax bill if you know it. That avoids confusion about whether you are in Upper Darby Township. We serve Upper Darby Township and our nearby town pages cover Clifton Heights and Darby.

While you wait

  • Find the main shutoffIt is usually where the water line enters the house near the meter. Turn it clockwise to stop a leak.
  • Open the lowest faucetThis drains the lines and lowers pressure while you wait.
  • Stay clear of wet electricsKeep away from outlets and the panel if the floor is wet.
  • Protect what you canLift rugs and boxes off the floor and take photos of any damage.
