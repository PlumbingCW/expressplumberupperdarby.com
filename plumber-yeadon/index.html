Yeadon at a glance

Yeadon was incorporated in 1893 and covers about 1.59 square miles. It has around 12,054 residents and 4,932 housing units. The borough borders Darby to the south, Lansdowne to the northwest, Upper Darby to the west and north and Philadelphia to the east across Cobbs Creek.

Landmarks include the Nile Swim Club, which opened in 1959, and Holy Cross Cemetery. US Route 13 runs through the borough. We serve Yeadon from the neighboring township of Upper Darby, so a plumber is close.
