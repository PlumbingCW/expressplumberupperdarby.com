The leak you have, not the leak you think you have

A drip from the spout is not the same as a leak under the sink, and neither is the same as water inside the wall. We ask where the water shows first, because it decides whether the repair is a cartridge, a washer, a supply line or a valve behind the wall.

Leaky faucet repair is one of the most searched plumbing fixes, and for good reason: a drip of a few drops a second adds up over a month. Most drips are worn internal parts, not a failed faucet.

Fixing a leaky faucet by type

Compression faucets

Two handles and rubber washers. Washers and seats wear out and cause a steady drip.

Cartridge faucets

A cartridge controls flow and temperature, and a worn one leaks or sticks.

Ball and disc faucets

Seals and springs wear, and the leak shows at the base or the spout.

Pull-down and kitchen faucets

Hoses, couplers and spray heads can leak under the sink.

We carry common cartridges and seals, and when a part is discontinued we tell you honestly if a replacement faucet is the better choice. A faucet that needs repeated repairs may not be worth fixing, and we tell you before you pay.

Tub faucets, diverters and shower valves

A tub spout that keeps running when the shower is on, a diverter that will not lift or a shower valve that drips can usually be fixed. A diverter that fails sends water to the spout when you want the showerhead, and replacing it or the spout is straightforward when the valve behind the wall is good.

If the valve behind the wall is the problem, access matters. In many older bathrooms it can be reached from a panel in an adjacent room or from the basement ceiling below. We tell you where we need to open before we start.

Supply valves and burst supply lines

The small chrome valves under a sink and behind a toilet are called angle stops. When they will not close, or when a braided supply line bursts, you have no easy way to stop the water. We replace stops and lines, and we recommend new stainless braided lines when old rubber or plastic ones show age.

If you cannot shut the water off at all, you may need the main, and the burst pipe repair page walks through the shutoff steps. When a leak turns out to be inside a wall or a ceiling, leak detection is the next step.

Shower and tub drain clogs

A shower or tub that drains slowly is usually hair and soap in the trap. We pull the strainer, clear the trap and cable the branch if needed. If it comes back quickly, the cause may be deeper in the line, and the drain cleaning page covers that.

Faucets in older Upper Darby bathrooms and kitchens

Fixtures in older homes can be decades old, with brass stems that are seized and shutoff valves that have never been turned. Forcing one can snap it, so we work carefully and sometimes replace the valve first. We tell you what we find under the sink or behind the tub before we start on the faucet itself.
