What the service line is

The service line runs underground from the water main, usually in the street, to your meter and into the house. Part of the line is often the utility's responsibility and part is yours. Who owns which part depends on your water utility and where the curb stop and the meter are. We help you work out where your side starts and tell you when the problem belongs to the utility.

In Upper Darby, a water main replacement project has been underway along parts of Township Line Road by the local water company, which shows that the utility is working on aging pipe in the area. Utility work does not tell you whether your own line is healthy, which is why a suspected leak still needs a proper check.

Signs of a service line problem

Wet or soft spot in the yard

Water surfacing between the street and the house.

Low pressure everywhere

A leak or a restriction reduces flow to the whole house.

Meter moving with everything off

Water is going somewhere, and the line is a prime suspect.

Unexplained high water bill

A hidden leak can run for weeks before it shows up outdoors.

To check the meter, turn off every fixture and appliance and watch the leak indicator or the dial. If it moves, water is leaking somewhere. Closing the main inside the house and watching again tells you if the leak is between the meter and the house or elsewhere.

Low water pressure

Low pressure has more than one cause. A partly closed valve, a failing pressure regulator, scaled pipe inside the house and a leaking service line all show up the same way at the faucet. We check pressure at an outdoor spigot first. If it is low there too, the problem is before the house plumbing. If it is fine there and low upstairs, the problem is in the house, and repiping may be part of the answer.

Meter, valves and the property line

Some parts of the system, such as the meter and the curb stop, belong to the water utility, and we do not touch them without the right permission. If we find the leak is on the utility side, we tell you and help you report it. Where it is on your side, we repair it. A related problem inside the walls is covered on the leak detection page.

Permits and restoration

Work in a driveway, sidewalk or street may need township permits. Ask the Upper Darby Department of Licenses and Inspection about what applies to your property. We explain what restoration is included in the scope, whether that is backfilling, patching or leaving the surface for a paving contractor.
