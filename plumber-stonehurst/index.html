Where Stonehurst is

Stonehurst is a neighborhood within Upper Darby Township. A local real estate guide places the combined Stonehurst and Cardington area in the northeast part of the township, separated from Philadelphia by Cobbs Creek, with Market Street, South 69th Street and Garrett Road as the main commercial streets. The township's fire service lists a Cardington-Stonehurst company. No official boundary is published that we could confirm, so we use those anchor streets.

Parks nearby include Cardington Recreation Area and 69th Street Recreation Park, which has a trail linking to Cobbs Creek Park. St. Demetrios Greek Orthodox Church and Stonehurst Hills Elementary are local landmarks.

Rowhouses and stone-faced homes

The guide describes mostly two-story rowhouses, many with stone faces, plus Tudor Revival homes and a few Colonial Revival houses just south of Market Street. Another guide says Stonehurst Hills has stone colonials, townhouses and rowhouses, many dating to the 1920s. These are descriptions from real estate sources, not an inspection of pipes.

Dense attached housing changes how plumbing calls work. Walls, drains and sometimes shutoff access are close to the next house. A leak behind one wall can reach the neighbor. We ask who shares the wall and where the shutoffs are before we start.

Calls that suit dense attached housing

  • A kitchen or basement drain that backs up when a neighbor runs water: drain cleaning and a camera inspection find where the clog is.
  • Water heaters in tight basements: replacement starts with checking space, venting and access.
  • A stain on a shared wall or ceiling: leak detection helps trace it without opening walls first.
  • Old supply lines that leak at joints: see repiping for when replacement makes sense.

Before we arrive

  • Find the main shutoffIt is usually where the water line enters the house near the meter. Turn it clockwise to stop a leak.
  • Open the lowest faucetThis drains the lines and lowers pressure while you wait.
  • Stay clear of wet electricsKeep away from outlets and the panel if the floor is wet.
  • Protect what you canLift rugs and boxes off the floor and take photos of any damage.
  • Have your address readyGive the street, the nearest cross street and the floor or unit if you are in Stonehurst.

If you share a wall with a neighbor and water is spreading, let them know too. It helps both of you.
