Upper Darby Township and its neighborhoods

Our home area is Upper Darby Township in Delaware County. We serve ZIP code 19082, which includes Terminal Square and much of the township, and ZIP code 19026 for Drexel Hill. Neighborhoods we visit often include Primos, Secane, Beverly Hills, Highland Park, Bywood, Stonehurst, Aronimink, Kirklyn and Westbrook Park.

The nearby boroughs and townships have their own pages: Lansdowne and Yeadon share ZIP code 19050, Clifton Heights uses 19018, Darby uses 19023 and Haverford Township uses 19083. We list these as nearby areas, and we ask for your address on the call to confirm that we can reach you.

How we decide what is nearby

These towns border Upper Darby Township or sit close to it, and a plumber can reach each one from our Garrett Road base without crossing into a different market. We estimate drive times on the call. Traffic on Route 1, State Road and the roads around the 69th Street Transportation Center changes through the day, so we keep those estimates honest.

If your address is outside these towns, call anyway. We tell you plainly whether we can reach you, and we do not list a town we cannot serve well.

What changes from town to town

The age of the housing is the biggest difference. Older boroughs such as Darby and Lansdowne have many houses built before the war, with galvanized or early copper supply pipe and cast iron drains. Haverford Township has postwar tracts with copper and a different layout. We ask for the house age on every call because it points us to the likely cause before we arrive.

Creeks also matter. Cobbs Creek runs along the eastern edge of Upper Darby and Yeadon, and Darby Creek runs through the area around Lansdowne, Clifton Heights and Darby. We do not claim that any street floods, but basement moisture calls come up more often near the water, and we check whether it is a pipe, a pump or groundwater.

Emergency help in every area

Our 24/7 line serves all of these towns on the same terms. We help you stop the water. We book the first available plumber. We give you a clear price before work starts. Gas smells go to the gas utility or 911 first, and we come once the house is cleared.

Before you call

  • Have your street address and the nearest cross street ready.
  • Know where your main water shutoff is, or tell us you cannot find it.
  • Tell us how old the house is and whether it is a rowhouse, a twin or a single home.
  • Tell us who can let the plumber in if you are not home.

Those four details speed up the call and let us send the right person with the right parts. If you are a landlord or property manager, tell us which unit is affected and whether the tenant knows we are coming.

Residents of Terminal Square, Primos, Secane and Drexel Hill can also read about their neighborhoods on our about page and in the neighborhood notes across our service pages.

Smaller places we also reach

Several smaller communities sit between the towns above, and we reach them from the same base. Millbourne lies beside Yeadon and Upper Darby, East Lansdowne adjoins Lansdowne, Folcroft is close to Darby and Glenolden is a short way down the road from Darby and Collingdale. We do not give each its own page, because their plumbing questions match the larger neighbor. You can call from any of them, and we will confirm that we can get to your address.

Housing in these places tends to be the same mix of older twins, rowhouses and small apartment buildings. A leak in one of them is handled the same way as anywhere else. We help you shut off the water, send the first available plumber and explain the repair before we start.

For emergency help anywhere in this area, call (215) 714-6692. Start with burst pipe repair if water is flowing, or see plumbing installation for planned work.
